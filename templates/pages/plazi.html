{{ tHTML "page.plazi.plaziTreatmentBankDealsWithScientific" "Plazi TreatmentBank deals with scientific, published, biosystematic literature — the corpus documenting all of the world's ca. 1.9 million known species across an estimated 500 million published pages. Each treatment is a well-defined part of an article that fixes the usage of a scientific name by an author at a given time; like bibliographic references, treatments can be cited and build on earlier ones. Together they represent the current scientific consensus on biological taxonomy, fully reference the underlying literature, and link to specimens and images archived at Zenodo/CERN. The cited publications are available through the Biodiversity Literature Repository." }}
