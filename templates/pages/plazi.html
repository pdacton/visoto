About Plazi

Plazi is a not-for-profit organisation that liberates taxonomic knowledge from the scientific literature. It converts descriptions of species — so-called treatments — from published articles into machine-readable, openly accessible data, and publishes them as linked data.

Every organism described in the literature is placed within a hierarchical classification. At its very top sit a handful of kingdoms that group all living things. Each kingdom below is represented by a TaxonName resource that you can explore in Visoto to drill down through the tree of life.

Animalia

Animals — multicellular organisms that feed on other organisms.

Plantae

Plants — mostly photosynthetic organisms, from mosses to flowering plants.

Fungi

Fungi — moulds, yeasts and mushrooms that absorb nutrients from their surroundings.

Bacteria

Bacteria — single-celled prokaryotic microorganisms found in nearly every habitat.

Chromista

Chromists — algae and protists such as diatoms, brown algae and water moulds.

Protozoa

Protozoa — single-celled eukaryotic organisms such as amoebae and flagellates.
