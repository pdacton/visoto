A demonstration of the sparqlAsyncTable partial in faceted mode over ZefixOrganisation (~790k registered Swiss companies). Each facetable column carries a filter control in its header (the funnel icon), driven by the <sparql-facet> declarations on this page. A selection filters the loaded rows instantly in the browser; because this class is too large to hold entirely, the app then quietly fetches the true full-class matches from the backend and swaps them in — you get an immediate preview and an authoritative result without ever confronting the size.

  • Legal form — a multi-select enum facet over schema:additionalType (values enumerated from the store, e.g. GmbH, AG, sole proprietorship); pick one or more, or (no value) for companies with no recorded legal form.
  • Name contains — a free-text facet over schema:name, with a (no value) option for companies with no recorded name.
  • Postal code — a numeric range over the property path schema:address/schema:postalCode (stored as strings, coerced for comparison), also with a (no value) option.

Because the class is large, the initial (unfiltered) view is capped for responsiveness — open a column's filter to narrow it.

SELECT ?org ?name ?legalForm ?postalCode WHERE { ?org a ?? .
