The FOPH COVID-19 graph is a sealed archive: the complete daily record of the pandemic in Switzerland — cases, deaths, hospitalizations, vaccinations — frozen at 1 January 2023, when mandatory daily reporting ended. The FONES supply-disruptions graph is its opposite, a living feed: every day the Federal Office for National Economic Supply publishes a fresh snapshot of which vital medicines are currently unavailable, and when they are expected back." }}

{{ tHTML "page.health.companiesThatMarketVitalMedicines" "Companies that market vital medicines for human use in Switzerland are legally required to report supply disruptions to the Federal Office for National Economic Supply. The graph turns those reports into a rolling series of daily snapshots: one observation is one medicine on one day, carrying its ATC code, package identifier (GTIN), the date the disruption began, the expected end, and a recommendation for prescribers. To see the situation today, you read the latest snapshot — which is exactly what the tables below do. A companion cube tracks just the daily totals of open and closed disruptions over time."
